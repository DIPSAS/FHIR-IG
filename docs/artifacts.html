Norwegian Implementations of Vitalsign

These are the Profiles of Vitalsigns

NoImplVitalSignsObservationBloodpressure

The local measurement of arterial blood pressure, which is a surrogate for arterial pressure in the systemic circulation.
NoImplVitalSignsObservationBodyHeight

Height, or body length, is measured from crown of head to sole of foot.
NoImplVitalSignsObservationBodyMassIndex

Calculated measurement which compares a person's weight and height.
NoImplVitalSignsObservationBodyTemp

A measurement of the body temperature, which is a surrogate for the core body temperature of the individual.
NoImplVitalSignsObservationBodyWeight

Measurement of the body weight of an individual.
NoImplVitalSignsObservationConsciousness

Simple scale used as part of an assessment to measure and record an individual's level of consciousness
NoImplVitalSignsObservationGCS

Fifteen point scale used to assess impairment of consciousness in response to defined stimuli. More correctly known as the Modified Glasgow coma scale.
NoImplVitalSignsObservationHeartRate

The rate and associated attributes for a heart beat.
NoImplVitalSignsObservationNews2Score

A simple assessment score used to identify clinical deterioration in a patient.
NoImplVitalSignsObservationOxygenSaturation

Blood oxygen and related measurements, measured by pulse oximetry or pulse CO-oximetry.
NoImplVitalSignsObservationPulse

The rate and associated attributes for a pulse.
NoImplVitalSignsObservationQSOFAScore

Quick Sepsis-related Organ Failure Assessment (qSOFA) is a simplified version of the SOFA score, which is used outside intensive care units to quickly assess sepsis risk in adults.
NoImplVitalSignsObservationRespirationRate

The characteristics of spontaneous breathing by an individual.
